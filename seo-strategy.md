# Dreamline Booking — SEO Keyword & Ranking Plan

## 1. Primary keywords (target on the homepage)
These are high-intent, medium-competition terms — put them in the title tag, H1, and first paragraph (already reflected in the current `<title>` and meta description).

- bus booking Kenya
- Dreamline bus booking
- Dreamline Express Kenya
- book bus ticket online Kenya
- Nairobi bus booking

## 2. Route-specific keywords (one per route page or FAQ block)
Google ranks route pages far better than a single homepage for searches like "Nairobi to Mombasa bus". Since every route currently lives inside one page, prioritize adding short on-page blurbs (or dedicated landing sections/pages) targeting:

- Nairobi to Mombasa bus booking
- Nairobi to Kisumu bus fare
- Nairobi to Eldoret bus timetable
- Nairobi to Kampala bus ticket
- Mombasa to Kisumu bus
- Nairobi to Kericho bus
- Nairobi to Bungoma bus
- Nairobi to Busia bus schedule
- Kampala to Mombasa bus Uganda
- VIP bus Nairobi Mombasa

**Tip:** a page (or a well-tagged `<section>` with its own H2/H3 and 150+ words) per top route out-performs one generic paragraph — this is the single highest-leverage change available.

## 7. Off-page / ranking factors that matter more than on-page copy
1. **Google Business Profile** — claim/verify listings for both Nairobi and Mombasa offices, matching NAP (Name, Address, Phone) exactly with the website footer. This is usually the single biggest lever for "bus booking near me" style searches.
2. **Backlinks** — get listed on Kenyan travel directories, Uganda–Kenya travel blogs, and local business directories (Yellow Pages Kenya, BusInfo-type aggregators). Ask satisfied customers to mention/link the site.
3. **Reviews** — encourage Google reviews at the booking offices; review count and rating strongly influence local pack rankings.
4. **Page speed & mobile usability** — run the live site through PageSpeed Insights once hosted; the current design is lightweight (no heavy JS frameworks), which helps.
5. **HTTPS + working canonical** — make sure `https://dreamlinebooking.com` (not `www.` unless you choose that as canonical) is the one consistent version everywhere: Google Business Profile, WhatsApp links, social bios.
6. **Consistent NAP everywhere** — phone numbers and addresses should match, character-for-character, across the website, Google Business Profile, and any directory listing.
7. **Submit to Google Search Console** — verify the domain, submit `sitemap.xml` (included in this delivery), and monitor for indexing errors.
