Questions travellers ask us

How do I book a seat?

Search your route above, open the booking form on the matching row, fill in your details and send it to the desk on WhatsApp. The desk replies with the departure time and your seat numbers.

How do I pay?

Only through the system-generated M-Pesa prompt sent after your seat is confirmed. Dreamline never asks you to send money to a personal number.

Can I send a parcel instead of travelling?

Yes. Parcels move on the same coaches. Drop yours at any Dreamline booking office and it is collected at the destination office.

Do you cross into Uganda?

Yes — Nairobi, Kisumu and Mombasa all connect to Kampala. Carry a valid passport or East African ID for the border post.

Can I change my travel date?

Message the desk that confirmed your booking as early as you can. Changes depend on seat availability on the new date.
