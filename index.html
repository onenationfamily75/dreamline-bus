Three steps from search to seat

Bookings are split between two desks by corridor, so the person replying to you already knows the road.

01

Questions travellers ask us

How do I book a seat?

Search your route above, open the booking form on the matching row, fill in your details and send it to the desk on WhatsApp. The desk replies with the departure time, your seat numbers and the final fare.

How much does a ticket cost?

Indicative Standard fares start from about KSh 600 (Mombasa to Malindi) and KSh 900 (Nairobi to Nakuru). Nairobi to Mombasa is from KSh 1,800 Standard and KSh 2,200 VIP, Nairobi to Kisumu from KSh 1,800, and Nairobi to Kampala from KSh 1,800. See the full list under Routes & fares. Prices rise on weekends, public holidays and school-term days, so the desk confirms the final fare before you pay.

How do I pay?

Only through the system-generated M-Pesa prompt sent after your seat is confirmed. Dreamline never asks you to send money to a personal number.

Can I send a parcel instead of travelling?

Yes. Parcels move on the same coaches. Drop yours at any Dreamline booking office and it is collected at the destination office.

Do you cross into Uganda?

Yes — Nairobi, Kisumu and Mombasa all connect to Kampala; Nairobi to Kampala is from KSh 1,800 Standard. Carry a valid passport or East African ID for the border post.

Can I change my travel date?

Message the desk that confirmed your booking as early as you can. Changes depend on seat availability on the new date, and the fare can differ if the new date is a peak day.
